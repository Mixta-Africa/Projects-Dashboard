Every construction milestone — clearing, sandfill, and the main civil works — depends on the account staying funded. 🔴 No new sales in 17 weeks — and nothing to show buyers yet
The project has not received any new buyer payments since January. The hospitality area has just been redesigned — adding new units is positive, but it has paused all sales activity because the marketing visuals (renders and the "coming soon" billboard) cannot be produced until the new design is finalised. There are no active offer letters, meaning no one is currently in the process of purchasing. Until the design is frozen and the renders are ready, the sales team has nothing to show new buyers.
● Needs action this week · High chance of continuing · Happening now
🟡 The rains are about to arrive and we're not done preparing the site
We are 44% through filling the site with sand and 56.5% through clearing the road layout. Both activities slow significantly in heavy rain, which typically begins in May. If clearing is paused for 4 or more weeks, the main contractor cannot mobilise even when the sales milestone is hit — pushing the whole project into a late-2026 start and a mid-2028 handover at best.
● Important · Almost certain to slow us down · 2–4 weeks away
🟡 We haven't decided which contractor will build the project yet
A decision is still pending at the DA level on whether BAFF or FUNKUS will be the main civil contractor. This means that even if we hit the ₦800M sales milestone tomorrow, we could not call a contractor on site — because we haven't formally chosen one. There are also recurring issues with smaller contractors (the BOLAD lights contractor is classified as "elusive and unresponsive") suggesting we need a clearer escalation process for contractor accountability.
● Important · Medium chance of resolution · 4–8 weeks to escalate
🔵 The redesign is good for the project — but the design needs to be signed off this week
Moving the hospitality area to a new location (CX10) adds 6 commercial spaces and 68 more residential units, which increases the project's total value significantly. But the architects cannot produce the final renders, and the billboard company cannot fabricate the "coming soon" board, until the design layout is formally frozen and signed off. Every week this is delayed is a week the sales team cannot actively market to new buyers. With the rainy season dampening buyer interest from May, there is a narrow window to get marketing live.
● Worth watching · Medium urgency · Decision needed in 2–3 weeks
